For developers

Get your app funded, without the campaign.

AppLov gives app developers rapid financing and resources with minimum effort. Investors share in your success through a transparent, automated revenue share.

How it works for developers

  1. Step 1
    List your app in the gallery
    The AppLov gallery showcases your passion and the potential of your work, and invites investors to share in your success.
  2. Step 2
    Get AI-backed analysis
    Our AI reviews your project and produces competitive analysis and revenue projections, so investors see a clear picture of your app.
  3. Step 3
    Receive funding
    Investors fund your app through a revenue share. Each investor's stake is represented by an NFT.
  4. Step 4
    Connect your app store
    App store integration lets AppLov see your app's revenue, so the revenue share is calculated and paid automatically.
  5. Step 5
    Ship, earn and share
    As your app earns, smart contracts distribute the investors' share. Payments to you are routed through our compliance mechanism.

What powers it

App gallery

A public showcase for your app, built to present your work and its potential to investors.

NFTs for stakes

Each investor's revenue share stake is an NFT, so ownership is clear, transferable and visible on the blockchain.

App store integration

Connecting your app store account lets revenue flow into the revenue share automatically, with no manual reporting.

Compliant payment routing

A proprietary financial routing mechanism ensures that payments to developers meet compliance requirements.

✨ AI

Predictions and analysis from a model we trained ourselves

AppLov's AI is an in-house trained and optimized model, built for app ventures. It screens projects and developers, and produces competitive analysis, insights and revenue predictions. For developers, that means a fast, consistent read on your app's viability and its competitors.

Predictions and projections are estimates, not guarantees.
