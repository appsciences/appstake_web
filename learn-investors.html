For investors

Invest early in apps, share in the revenue.

applov is early stage, AI-powered, blockchain-automated revenue share investing. Back app ventures you believe in and share in their success.

How it works for investors

  1. Step 1
    Browse the app gallery
    Explore apps and developers showcased in the applov gallery, each presented with its potential and its team.
  2. Step 2
    Review AI insights and predictions
    Our AI screens every project and developer and provides insights, competitive analysis and revenue predictions to support your decision.
  3. Step 3
    Invest and receive an NFT
    Fund an app through a revenue share. Your stake is represented by an NFT recorded on the blockchain.
  4. Step 4
    Revenue tracked through the app store
    App store integration lets applov see the app's revenue, so there is a reliable basis for your share.
  5. Step 5
    Share in the success
    Smart contracts automate the revenue share and distribute your portion as the app earns, transparently on the blockchain.

What powers it

Blockchain transparency

Stakes, revenue and distributions are recorded on the blockchain, so you can see what is happening with your investment.

NFT ownership

Your revenue share stake is an NFT that you hold, so ownership is clear and verifiable.

Automated revenue share

Smart contracts automate distributions, which removes manual accounting from the process.

Payment compliance

A proprietary financial routing mechanism ensures that payments to developers meet compliance requirements.

✨ AI

Predictions from a model we trained ourselves

applov's AI is an in-house trained and optimized model, built for app ventures. It screens projects and developers, and produces competitive analysis, insights and revenue predictions. For investors, that means project and developer screening, plus insights and projections you can weigh yourself.

Predictions and projections are estimates, not guarantees.
